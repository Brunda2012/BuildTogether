# Persona Profile: Bori – The Resource / Individual Contributor

Nov 21, 2026

Bori is a Copenhagen-based finance professional in their mid-30s who leads AI adoption at work and wants to build something of their own outside it. Bori represents the **Resource / Individual** role on the platform: someone with skills and limited time who wants to join an early-stage project, or become a co-founder.

## Persona snapshot

| Attribute | Bori |
| --- | --- |
| Platform role | Resource / Individual contributor (potential co-founder) |
| Age | Mid-30s |
| Location | Copenhagen, Denmark |
| Occupation | Finance professional in a corporate setting; leads AI adoption initiatives; builds AI-powered apps through vibe coding and experimentation |
| Education | Master's degree, Copenhagen Business School (CBS) |
| Work pattern | Standard corporate 9-to-5; side projects and entrepreneurship outside work |
| Time for side projects | About 1-2 hours per day, more if a project shows momentum |
| Tech comfort | Very high: builds applications, uses AI daily, enjoys emerging tools |
| Ideal project role | Builder, product creator, AI enabler, problem solver, potential co-founder |
| Geography | Strong preference for Copenhagen and Denmark |

> "I don't need another networking event. I need a community where I can discover interesting people and projects that match my interests, and where building something together feels natural."

## Goals and motivations

Bori wants to find trustworthy collaborators and turn an idea into a viable business or side venture, ultimately creating something meaningful outside corporate life.

| | What it looks like for Bori |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | Find like-minded people, build a product or business, explore startup opportunities |
| Success | Trustworthy collaborators; joining or creating a promising project; an idea becoming a viable venture |
| Motivators | Entrepreneurship, innovation, learning, creativity, meeting interesting people, building something impactful |
| Decision drivers | Shared interests, quality of the people involved, project relevance, authenticity, potential for real collaboration |
| Environment | Stable corporate job, with active exploration of entrepreneurship outside it |

## The business problem

**Bori has no trusted, local place to discover the right people, projects and opportunities, so finding them costs hours across disconnected platforms and often leads nowhere.**

Relevant events, hackathons, communities and project opportunities are scattered across LinkedIn, networking groups, hackathon sites, Meetup pages and assorted communities. With only 1-2 hours a day, Bori spends much of that time searching and evaluating rather than building.

### How Bori solves it today, and why it falls short

| Current approach | Why it is insufficient |
| --- | --- |
| Monitoring LinkedIn | Weak discovery; superficial networking; no matching on project goals or skills |
| Following networking groups and organisations | Generic events; exclusive or cliquish cultures; low relevance |
| Searching hackathon listings and event platforms | Fragmented, manual, hard to compare |
| Attending events and exploring communities | Time-consuming, with no guarantee of meeting the right people |

### Where the time goes

- Searching for relevant events and project opportunities
- Evaluating whether a community is worth joining
- Identifying suitable collaborators
- Comparing opportunities across multiple platforms

### Risks and concerns

- Investing time in low-quality communities
- Scam-like platforms
- Superficial networking
- Projects that never move beyond the idea phase

**Biggest unmet need:** a trusted local platform that connects Bori with the right people, projects, ideas and opportunities without extensive searching across multiple platforms.

## Behaviours, preferences and trust

Bori wants curated, context-rich introductions from a friendly, authentic platform, and distrusts anything that feels spammy, cliquish or over-styled.

| Dimension | Bori's preference |
| --- | --- |
| Usage frequency | Browses opportunities, events and communities regularly through the week |
| Receiving information | Curated, personalised recommendations rather than manual searching through large volumes |
| Connecting with people | Through shared interests and established common ground, ideally via a digital platform before meeting in person |
| Ideal first interaction | Shared interests and complementary goals visible before meeting, so the conversation starts with context |
| People vs algorithms | Trusts both equally, provided the results are good |
| Tone that resonates | Friendly, informal, professional, approachable, authentic, community-oriented |
| Tone that puts Bori off | Overly complicated, overly trendy, overly corporate, spammy, or Gen Z-styled communication that lacks authenticity |
| Community that attracts | Open, welcoming, collaborative, where people genuinely want to build together |
| Community that intimidates | Exclusive groups with insider culture, heavy jargon, internal jokes, cliquish behaviour |
| Trust builders | Transparency, quality profiles, authentic communities, meaningful matches, local relevance, professional presentation |

## Key platform criteria from Bori's perspective

**Bori will adopt and stay on the platform only if it delivers high-quality matches, with local relevance, from one trusted place.** The criteria below are grouped by how decisive they are.

### Must-haves

| # | Criterion | What Bori needs | Pain it removes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Intelligent matching | Matches on interests, goals, skills and project ambitions | No trusted way to match people today |
| 2 | Centralised discovery | People, projects, communities and events in one place | Scattered across LinkedIn, Meetup, hackathon sites and groups |
| 3 | Local relevance | Denmark and Copenhagen first, with location filtering | Global noise; hard to find local people and projects |
| 4 | Personalised recommendations | Curated suggestions pushed to Bori, not manual search | Hours of searching in a 1-2 hour daily window |
| 5 | Effective filtering | Narrow by role, stage, skills, commitment and location | Hard to compare opportunities across platforms |
| 6 | Project discovery | Browse early-stage projects and open roles, including co-founder roles | Projects hard to find; many never leave the idea phase |

### Trust builders

- Quality user profiles and verified, authentic members
- Transparent matching logic and clear project information
- Professional but approachable presentation
- Active, welcoming communities rather than exclusive circles

### Delighters

- Highly relevant people and projects surfaced with minimal effort
- Shared context and complementary goals visible before a first conversation
- A community feel where building together comes naturally

### Churn triggers

- No meaningful matches, or poor recommendations
- Low user quality, or scam-like and superficial activity
- Outdated user experience
- Little active community engagement

## Implications for the platform

Bori is the supply side of the platform: the resource that project leads and founders need, and whose participation gives investors early-stage teams worth backing.

- **Match quality is the product.** Bori's trust depends on profiles, matching and recommendations being good enough to replace manual searching.
- **Supply quality gates trust.** Bori fears scam-like platforms and projects that stall, so founder and project listings need vetting signals as much as individual profiles do.
- **Local density comes first.** A Copenhagen and Denmark launch focus fits Bori's strong geographic preference and keeps the community active.
- **Time-boxed engagement.** With 1-2 hours a day, the experience should reward short sessions: recommendations first, search second.
- **Tone is a design requirement.** Friendly, authentic and professional; no corporate stiffness, no trend-chasing.
