Hey team! I suggest we use a practical business-world example to demonstrate BuildTogether.

**Meet Bruno – Founder & Technical Co-founder**

Bruno is building a startup called **AuditSphere AI**, an AI-powered audit and investigation intelligence platform.

The platform helps organisations analyse financial transactions, identify unusual patterns, organise investigation evidence and streamline internal audit workflows.

Bruno has already developed an MVP, but he faces several challenges:

- He needs a business-oriented co-founder with enterprise sales experience.
- He needs investors interested in AI, RegTech and enterprise software.
- He needs internal audit and investigation experts to validate his product.
- He needs enterprise companies willing to pilot his solution.
- He needs guidance on security, compliance and scaling.

**How BuildTogether helps Bruno:**

1. Bruno creates his founder and startup profiles.
2. BuildTogether analyses his startup stage, capabilities and business requirements.
3. AI identifies gaps in his startup, such as enterprise sales, domain expertise and customer validation.
4. It recommends a potential business co-founder, relevant investors and audit experts.
5. It explains why each match is relevant and allows Bruno to request introductions.

**The three personas:**

- **Founder – Bruno:** Looking for the right people, funding and resources to grow his startup.
- **Investor:** Looking for promising early-stage AI and enterprise software startups.
- **Resource Provider:** An audit expert, mentor or consultant looking for startups that need their expertise.

**What makes our idea different?**

We are not just building another networking or co-founder matching platform. We want BuildTogether to understand what a startup is missing and recommend the right people, capital and resources based on its actual needs.

For example, Bruno might think he needs funding, but our platform could identify that he first needs an audit-domain expert and an enterprise pilot customer to strengthen his business.

**Our central message:**

"BuildTogether doesn't just help founders find people. It helps them discover what their startup needs next and connects them with the right ecosystem to grow."
