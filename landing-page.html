Not another networking platform

You may think you need funding. You may first need a pilot customer.

Most platforms show you a list of people and leave the thinking to you. BuildTogether starts with your startup: its stage, what you can already do, and what is missing.

It then names the gap and introduces you to the person who closes it, with one clear reason why you two should talk.

How it works

From profile to introduction in three steps

1

Create your profile

Sign up as a founder, co-founder, contributor or investor. A first description is written for you, and you edit it until it sounds like you.

2

See what you need next

The AI reads your stage and your gaps, and tells you which person would move your startup forward right now.

3

Meet your matches

Go through your matches one at a time and say yes or not now. Each one comes with the reason you fit. When both say yes, you are introduced.
