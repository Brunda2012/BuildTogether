```
Build a profile page for our app "Sammen", matching the attached screenshot as closely as possible.

Style: minimalist Copenhagen look. Cream background (#F6F1E7), navy text (#16233F), white cards with a thin warm-grey border (#D9D2C3), one orange accent (#B4460A) for the main button and small labels. Font: Instrument Sans. Rounded corners, lots of white space, no gradients, no emojis.

Layout: two columns on desktop, stacked on mobile.

Left column:
- Portrait photo (attached portrait image), rounded corners
- Name "Mette" with a small "Investor" pill
- Subtitle "Business angel · Copenhagen"
- Card "Verified by Sammen" with five check marks: Identity, Business email, Company and track record, Vouched for by a member, Video introduction

Right column:
- Label "Looking for" and the large quote: "I back first-time founders in Europe who already have a working product and need their first cheque."
- Navy card "Why you two should meet" with a short text explaining the match. This text will later come from the AI matching, so make it a variable.
- "About" paragraph: "Sold her own company. Now invests her own money in early-stage startups, in any sector. No team and no analyst: she meets every founder herself."
- Four small fact cards in a 2x2 grid: Invests in / Early stage, any sector. Role in a round / First cheque. Background / Founder with an exit. Meets / In person or by video.
- Card "It starts with a coffee" with the attached café image and the text: "No pitch deck needed. If you both say yes, Sammen books the time: in a café in Copenhagen or on a video call."
- Two buttons: primary orange "Meet Mette for a coffee", secondary outlined "Not now"

Make the profile data come from one profile object, so the same page works for other people.
```
