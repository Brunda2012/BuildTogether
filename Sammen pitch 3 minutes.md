## Script

I am a mountain rescuer. In a rescue, two weeks late does not exist.

In venture capital, two weeks late is normal. Investors meet the best founders after the company exists, after the first deck, after five other investors have had the coffee.

But there is a moment before that. Someone leaves a job, a PhD or a contract. For a few weeks, they are free, and nobody can find them, because there is no company to find.

I know that moment. My contract ended one week ago. The next day, I started two companies.

And right now, that moment is happening to thousands of people. In Basel, Novartis alone is cutting close to 700 jobs. The University of Basel Innovation Office told me they see experienced people, even executives, who now have to reskill, and they run workshops to bring them into entrepreneurship.

At the same time, Philipp Baaske, the CEO of NanoTemper, says: Germany has no natural resources. What we have are big, beautiful, but empty buildings.

So we have the people. We have the buildings. What is missing is the connection, at the right moment.

That is Sammen. Danish for together.

Founders-to-be can be spotted before they found. We look at four signals: they have built before, they have deep expertise, their path is not a straight line, and they have just become available.

Sammen does three things. Spot: we read these signals in the fields an investor cares about, starting with robotics, defence and dual-use, AI and biotech. Invite: both sides say yes before anyone is introduced. Meet: twenty minutes, over a coffee, in person or on video.

Investors see founders first. Founders-to-be are seen at the right moment, without a pitch deck.

We started yesterday. Our survey went out to my network of almost 9,000 followers, one in four of them director level or above. The answers told us the same thing from both sides: people cannot find each other, and they do not know who is serious.

Our model: founders-to-be join free. Investors pay for access to their sectors.

What we ask today: ten investors and ten founders-to-be for a pilot in Copenhagen, Munich and Basel.

The next unicorn is already out there. The buildings are waiting.

Let's find it, and fill them with life. Together.
