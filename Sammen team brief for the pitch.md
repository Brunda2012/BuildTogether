# Sammen: team brief for the pitch

Nov 22, 2026 · @Steffi

## The pitch in one sentence

Sammen brings investors and founders-to-be to one table, at the moment it matters.

**Who we serve first:** investors who want to meet strong founders before anyone else does. Founders-to-be are the other side of the table.

**What we no longer pitch today:** job seekers, employers, co-founder matching, the European angle. The mentors were clear: everyone is not a target group, and the jury will keep about three ideas.

## The three ideas the jury should remember

1. Investors meet the best founders two weeks too late.
2. Founders-to-be can be spotted before they found, through four signals.
3. Sammen gets both to one table: spot, invite, meet.

## The four signals, and what the product should show

| Signal | Evidence | Profile field in the app | What the investor sees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Has built before | About half of unicorn founders had started a business before (Endeavor 2023; Defiance Capital 2024) | Projects, GitHub link, hackathons | "Built 3 things, 1 hackathon win" |
| Deep expertise | About 10 years of work before founding; European unicorns with a PhD founder doubled from 9% to 18% (Endeavor; Revelio Labs with Accel 2026) | Field, years, strongest credential | "8 years in proteomics, Max Planck" |
| Non-linear path | 60% of unicorn founders studied or worked abroad (Endeavor) | Countries, field changes | "3 countries, moved from lab to software" |
| Just became available | Our hypothesis, not proven by data | "Available since" date, reason optional | "Available since 16 Nov" in orange |

Keep it to these four. The mentors asked for three or four strong signals, not a long list.

## Demo flow for the pitch, 60 seconds

1. **Investor view:** a short list of founders-to-be, each with the four signals as small tags. The newest "just became available" profile sits at the top.
2. **One profile:** verified badge, the four signals, one line in the person's own words.
3. **One action:** "Invite to the table" and a confirmation with a date for a coffee or a founder dinner.

If only one screen works by pitch time, make it the investor view.

## Numbers we can quote

**LinkedIn post, 21 to 22 November:** 1,859 impressions, 1,010 people reached, 83 interactions, 32 private shares, 8 clicks on the survey link. About 27% of viewers are directors, owners, managing directors, VPs or partners. Top locations: Munich 15%, Copenhagen 12%.

**Survey, 21 November, 12 responses in about seven hours:**

- 7 of 12 say only a few of their meetings are worth the time
- 9 of 12 want verified identity
- 8 of 12 want mutual interest before any contact
- Of the 4 founders looking for a first investor, 3 have never contacted one
- Founders would pay about €30 to €50; one wrote that a paid introduction "would not feel genuine"

**Check before quoting:** three responses (16:23, 16:55, 17:34) are almost identical, and so are two more (15:22, 15:37). If these were our own tests, the real sample is about eight. Say "12 people in seven hours", never percentages.

## Questions the jury may ask

**Where do the signals come from?** From public profiles and from what people enter themselves, with their consent. Nobody is listed without opting in.

**Why would a founder-to-be join?** It is free, and they meet investors and other builders before they have a company. In our survey, three of four founders seeking a first investor had never contacted one.

**Why you?** We are the profile. We met on Friday and had a product by Sunday.

**Is the "just became available" signal proven?** No. Three signals come from founder data. The fourth is our hypothesis and the first thing the pilot tests.

**How do you make money?** Investors pay for curated, early introductions. Founder dinners come first, a fund later.
